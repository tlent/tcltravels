Reserving a Flight
To reserve a specific flight listed under a flight listing, such as our recommendations of flights for you simply press the "reserve" button next to the flight listing. From here you are given the options of selecting additional passengers to purchase reservations for. In addition you must enter the first, last, and class of ticket for each passenger (including yourself). After submitting your all ready to go and the reservation will appear in the reservations tab.
Bidding on a Flight
To participate in a reverse auction, a process by which you get to pick the price of one of the given flights, simply press the bid button.Unlike reserving a flight this can not be done for other people and your flight information (including first, last, meals, class of ticket) must be entered. Most importantly you must place your bid, which upon submitting will be processed by our database and determine whether your bid was selected or rejected.
Changing Your Info
If you wish to change any relevant information that you entered when registering please click on your username in the upper right. Clicking on the edit button will allow you to change your information, if you wish to not make any changes or undo what you have typed press cancel. Else submit your changes with the submit button and the information will be updated.
Making a Reservation
If you wish to make a more custom Reservation, then go to the reservation tab and use the Make a reservation feature to construct a reservation leg by leg. First you must enter the airports for destination and origin of your reservation. You must also select the time from which you will leave and then submit the form. From there you will be directed to a page that will handle the construction of your reservation path.



Add and Edit Employees
To add a new employee or make a change to an existing employee go to the Admin panel and press the add or change employee button. This will take you a form which can be changed a re-submitted.
Get a sales Report by Month
If you wish to view a specifics sales report select a month and year and press view sales report. This will show the desired sales report for the time entered.
Get a sales Report by Flight
If you wish to view a specifics sales report for a flight select from the drop-down on the Sales Report table and press view sales report. This will show the desired sales report for the flight.
Get a sales Report by Customer
If you wish to view a specifics sales report for a customer then select the customer from the drop-down menu and press view sales report. This will show the desired sales report for the flight.
Get a sales Report by City
If you wish to view a specifics sales report for a city then select the city from the drop-down menu in the sales report table and press the view sales report button. This will show the desired sales report for the flight.
